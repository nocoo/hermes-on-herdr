Speech was synthesized locally from the pinned licensed model/voice files. No human narration recording was downloaded, and no person-specific voice cloning was performed for this production. Each of the 20 WAV files has an adjacent JSON record with original text, performance spelling, phonemes, generation settings and SHA-256. These records describe this generation process, not an independently audited history of the upstream training data.

The Hermes image is the official desktop `nous-girl.jpg` badge, referenced by the publisher's [BrandMark component](research/logos/hermes-brand-mark.tsx) at `436ec489854b7110b4c5506b1f52c514fa4c3ace`. The [Hermes MIT notice](research/logos/Hermes-LICENSE.txt) and [Pi MIT notice](research/logos/Pi-LICENSE.txt) are retained separately from trademark attribution. The earlier Hermes SVG remains identified as its documentation favicon, not a fabricated vector version of the desktop badge.

Installed tool dependencies retain their own license notices. The shared kit's CREDITS also documents its vendored PptxGenJS package and dependencies. Chrome, FFmpeg and the large TTS/ASR models are external tools or ignored caches; their binaries are not distributed in this production commit.
