# English narration

## intro

Hermes on Herdr.

## context

An independent Hermes can receive your messages.

But it does not automatically inherit your Herdr session's pane, socket, or caller context.

## talent

You trust brilliant coding partners: Codex, Grok, Pi, Claude Code.

As your workflow grows, coordination becomes the job.

## bind

The plugin makes the link explicit.

Choose a dedicated Hermes profile you trust, and bind it to your current Herdr session.

## managers

Hermes becomes your M2, with full Herdr control in the bound session.

It manages Herdr. Herdr coordinates your coding agents.

## channels

Hermes already supports Discord, Telegram, and Slack.

Configure a channel to reach your M2 from outside, while Herdr control stays local.

## local

No SSH. No remote desktop. No VPN or exposed Herdr control port.

Keep the host, Herdr, and gateway online.

## monitor

The plugin ties the dedicated gateway to Herdr's lifecycle.

Pause stays paused until you resume. The monitor TUI shows its state.

## trust

Herdr and plugin boundaries still apply.

Session binding is not an operating-system sandbox. Same-user terminal permissions remain broad.

## weekend

Let the system manage the talent.

Keep your judgment. Take your weekend back.
