# Review integration

The Herdr Grok review is preserved in `grok-review.md`. It is advisory research, not a permission rule or a verified legal opinion.

Accepted: distinguish M2 as an organizational metaphor; retain the explicit dedicated/trusted profile setup; do not imply profile isolation or a new access-control tier; make lifecycle startup conditional on running intent; preserve pause; label the TUI demo and messaging illustration; state that the machine and session must remain online. No untouched design proposal is advertised as a tested release feature.

For voices, the reviewer correctly distinguished a model's Apache license from a separately signed speaker release and noted that Edge TTS's client license is not a voice-distribution grant. We use the published Apache-licensed TTS models, the publisher's explicit production/commercial-use statement and original scripts. We do not scrape a human recording, identify a real performer, imitate a known person, claim a separate speaker contract, or present the voices as Microsoft licensed. `LICENSES.md` states this actual permission basis and reproduces the provenance evidence without promising warranties that the license does not provide. This meets the requested commercial TTS fallback; no new user approval was required for the already authorized production.

Visual review of the first actual renders found lower-node occlusion and crowded status labels. IC nodes were raised, tiny labels shortened, fan routes changed to clean Bezier curves, and status labels moved inside their terminal panels. The monitor is shown alone before the illustrative messaging panel enters. The initial contact sheet and source checkpoint are preserved under `process/`.

The second review caught pronunciation expansions leaking into English captions. The English performance script remains unchanged, while caption generation now writes `M2` and `TUI`; the English final MP4 was rerendered. The concise English closing headline was kept and moved into the story manifest so the film, chapter list and slide headings share one source. Actual decoded caption frames at 1604 and 2540 verify both corrected spellings and clear placement. The Chinese caption frame at 83 seconds also fits below the monitor scene.
